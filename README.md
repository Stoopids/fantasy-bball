# Fantasy BBALL NCAA Full Season and NCAA Tournament

A static, accounts-free web app for running a friends' fantasy draft of NCAA basketball teams. One commissioner sets up a league in a guided wizard, shares a link, managers join from their phones, and everyone drafts live in a shared room. One draft covers both the full season and the tournament. After the draft a league site shows standings (Full Season and NCAA Tournament), team cards and scores.

No server of our own: every page is plain HTML/JS/CSS and all shared state lives in [textdb.dev](https://textdb.dev). It is built to be hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Using it
A one-page plain-words guide for commissioners and managers ships with the app: `help.html` (linked from the home page).


**Commissioner** — open `index.html` → *Create a league*. One question per screen with Back/Next and a progress bar: league and your names (every league has exactly **8 managers**, a fixed number with nothing to choose), draft style (Snake default, or Linear), draft time (a date-time picker in your own zone, stored as UTC, or "Start now, I'll press Start"), start mode, who picks first (Random, or "I'll set the exact order"), pick clock (30/60/90/120/180 s or none; default 60 s), teams per manager = rounds (default 8), and what happens when time runs out (auto-pick on by default). There is no passcode to invent: the app makes a random commissioner secret for you. A summary screen lets you edit any answer before anything is created. You then get a league code, an invite link, a draft-room link, a read-only spectator link, your **private commissioner link** and **private manager link** (both keep their secret after a `#`, are masked on screen and must be saved), an invite QR, a **Copy reminder message** button (league name, draft time in UTC, a link that shows each person's local time, the invite link) and **Share** where the Web Share API exists. Save the links: the passcode is stored only as a salted hash and cannot be recovered.
In the lobby you can edit settings, reorder managers (drag the ⠿ handle or tap ▲ ▼), **Randomize**, spin the wheel (everyone with the room open sees the spin and the result in a full-screen overlay that closes by itself or on tap; the wheel can be used **once**: after a finished spin the button is gone, and a spin may be repeated only if the previous one never finished, i.e. nothing happened for 60 seconds), mark a manager away, rename or remove one, then **Start draft** (the button, the timed auto-start and the commissioner's *Start the draft now* all need **exactly 8 managers** joined; with fewer the lobby says *Waiting for N more managers* and nothing can start; there is no “start anyway” and no test mode in the shipped app). If every spin result is saved but the order was never applied (the commissioner's page closed or the save failed), the lobby button reads **Finish setting the order** and applying it is the only option; a second spin is never offered after that. Once live: pause/resume, undo the last pick, change the clock, toggle auto-pick, end the draft early (typed confirmation), reset. Order is editable until the draft starts, then locked. Commissioner actions need the commissioner link on a device: open it, or paste it under More → Unlock (the creating phone is already unlocked). While a draft runs, auto-start and auto-pick only happen while at least one phone or computer has the room open; the commissioner sees that said in plain words, with a one-tap *Start the draft now* / *Make the auto-pick now* button if a tab was closed when the time came.

**Manager** — open the invite link (or type the code on the home page): team name, your name, optional photo (cropped and shrunk on your phone to a small square under 25 KB; emoji or initial otherwise). In the room: Board, Teams (search, sort & group, ⭐ Starred, Q+ queue), My Team (queue and auto-pick switch), Chat (separate from the pick feed), More. A 15-second warning, vibration and sound alert you; missed picks are auto-picked from your queue, then the best power rank. Your private manager link (`room.html?l=…#as=…&k=…`) restores your identity on another phone. There is no name picker: a device can only act as a manager if it holds that manager's secret, and a removed manager gets a plain "you were removed" notice with no pick or chat controls.

**Spectator** — open the spectator link (`room.html?l=<code>&watch=1`). Read-only board, picks and chat. Anyone joining after the draft started is offered the spectator view; the order is fixed at the start.

After the draft: final rosters and recap (`recap.html?l=<code>`), CSV and JSON export.

## How it works

Pages: `index`, `help` (one-page guide: Commissioner / Managers), `setup` (wizard), `join`, `room`, `recap`, `league` (the league site, see below), `time` (shows a draft time in the viewer's zone). Modules: `js/core.js` (all pure logic, unit-tested, loadable in Node), `js/store.js` (the **only** module that talks to textdb — swap it for Supabase later), `js/ui.js`, `js/league.js` (`FBNLeague.load(id)` reads every league key once and returns the derived state), `js/scoring.js` (pure scoring, loadable in Node), `js/setup.js`, `js/join.js`, `js/room.js`, `js/recap.js`, `js/leaguehome.js`.

textdb.dev only offers GET and POST (POST replaces a whole document, last write wins, no compare-and-swap). So concurrency is handled by key layout and a verify step:
- **One key per pick slot**; the first valid entry wins when the log is replayed. A writer reads, writes, waits 600 ms, re-reads and learns whether it landed or was beaten. Two phones picking at once cannot double-pick.
- **One key per manager slot** (first write wins, same verify) so simultaneous joins never share a seat. Removing a manager bumps the slot's generation.
- Every writer owns its own key (profile, presence, queue/auto prefs), so there are no cross-writer overwrites. Chat merges by message id. Commissioner changes go through read-modify-write with verification and retry.
- Timeout/away auto-picks use deterministic entry ids, so several phones auto-picking write the same pick.
- Polling is light and only runs in a visible tab: about 4 s in the lobby, 2.5 s while a draft is live, 15 s once it is complete, with jitter, exponential back-off (up to 30 s) after errors, and the Offline pill clears at once when any request succeeds or the browser reports it is online again, claims/profiles/chat on slower staggered schedules, presence every 45 s; a hidden tab stops polling and catches up the moment it is shown again.
- **Every pick is a compare-and-set.** A pick records the pick number and the id of the previous pick; entries chained to a pick that was later undone are ignored, and *Undo* carries the pick number and id the commissioner was looking at and is refused if the board moved.

### Key schema (`<id>` = league code, lower-case, 16 chars / 80 bits; prefix `fbn1-`)
| key | holds |
|---|---|
| `fbn1-<id>-meta` | settings, status, order, epoch, voided picks, overrides (away/left/rename), slot generations, commissioner secret as salt + SHA-256 hash (never the secret) |
| `fbn1-<id>-s<i>-g<gen>` | claim of manager slot *i*: manager id, name, team, salted hash of that manager's personal secret |
| `fbn1-<id>-m-<mid>` | manager profile incl. the photo data URL (≤ 25 KB target, 40 KB hard cap) |
| `fbn1-<id>-e<epoch>-p<n>` | pick *n* of the current draft epoch |
| `fbn1-<id>-chat` | last 100 chat messages and reactions |
| `fbn1-<id>-u-<mid>` / `-pr-<mid>` | that manager's queue + auto-pick flag / presence heartbeat |
| `fbn1-<id>-e<epoch>-g<gen>-s<n>` | spin-wheel results |
| `fbn1-<id>-rd-<managerId>` | an owner's redraft choices (drops and, for last place, the pick position) |

textdb limits verified with throwaway keys: POST works up to 1,000,000 bytes (413 at 1,100,000); keys of 100 and 200 characters work, 400 does not; a GET of an unknown key creates it empty; Date headers are not readable cross-origin (time sync uses a HEAD on the page's own origin).

## Hosting on GitHub Pages (new repo or a sub-folder)
This app is a separate static site. It does not touch, import from or link to any existing site; host it in its **own** new repo, or in its own sub-folder of a repo you already control, never by editing existing pages.
1. Easiest: use the upload bundle `fantasy-bball-upload/` (shipped files only, with `MD5SUMS.txt`). Create a new repo and copy the bundle's contents to its root (or to a folder such as `/fantasy-bball/` inside an existing Pages repo). The bundle contains `index.html`, `setup.html`, `join.html`, `room.html`, `recap.html`, `league.html`, `time.html`, `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `css/`, `js/`, `assets/`, `data/` and `icons/`. `LICENSE-third-party.txt` and `tools/refresh_data.py` (the data refresh script) are included too. `tests/`, `shots/` and the notes are not needed to run it.
2. Settings → Pages → deploy from the branch root (or from the folder).
3. Open `https://<user>.github.io/<repo>/` (or `.../<folder>/`).
All links are relative, so any base path works with no changes; the generated invite/room links use the page's own location. The service worker (`sw.js`, cache name built from `js/version.js` so every build gets a fresh cache and old ones are deleted; network-first with a 4 s timeout, then the saved copy, then a plain offline page) is registered as `sw.js` next to the page, so its scope is that folder only; it ignores other origins (textdb, ESPN logos). Verified by serving the bundle from `/fantasy-bball/` with `python3 -m http.server` (see FINAL-TEST.md).
Before the real season: run `python3 tools/refresh_data.py` (see "League site") and commit the new `data/` files. Until then a real league's League site is already populated (every manager 0-0, T-1) and shows no scores.

## League site (`league.html?l=<code>`)
The button, link and heading are called exactly **League site** everywhere (room header pill, end-of-draft screen, recap, index, help). **The page exists by itself the moment the draft is complete**, for every manager, with no commissioner step: it is built in the browser from the league's own draft data (owners with emoji and photo, team names, rosters), so on day one every manager is on a card with 0 points and the rank T-1 (all tied), each with their 6 schools as chips (0-0). While the draft is still running the link shows *Available after the draft* and re-checks every 15 s, so an open page fills itself in. Look (`css/league-app.css`, `js/leaguehome.js`, pure data builder `js/league-d.js`): a banner photo with rounded corners, a big yellow league title, a *Standings updated … ↻ Refresh Live Games* line, a tagline, a one-row pill tab bar (short labels with icons on a phone, where the bar scrolls sideways inside itself and keeps the open tab in view; full labels on a desktop): **Standings, Games, Rosters, Power rankings, All teams, Postseason, Jan 20, 2027 redraft, Archive, Rules**, then **Draft** (a link to the draft room with the league code only, so identity and the commissioner secret stay on the device). **Draft recap** is linked in the footer. Standings are dark owner cards: rank (T-n for ties), emoji, manager and team name, big points, and a 4 x 2 grid of school chips (team colour, logo, name, W-L). Tap an owner for the full breakdown (Rosters tab). Every tab has a plain empty state before the season. A fixed chat bar at the bottom previews the last chat message and who is online; the chat itself stays in the draft room (it links to the room's Chat tab). Owner and team names show without a leading "Test " (display only, in every tab; the stored data is untouched; a name that is just "Test" or "Test Test" is shown as written, never empty). **Tap targets:** on a phone every tappable element (tab tiles, school bubbles, owner buttons, footer links, selects, rows) is at least 44 x 44 CSS px. **Completion** is derived from the picks (all 8 x rounds made, or ended early, or a redraft started), not from `meta.status`, so the page appears even while the room still stores `live`.

**Store errors never leave the League page dead (s18).** If the store cannot be reached (HTTP 503, network, timeout) the page says "Having trouble reaching the league. Retrying…" with a **Try now** button (44 px), retries by itself with growing waits (about 2 s, 4 s, 8 s, capped at 30 s, with jitter) and keeps polling. Before the draft it restores the "Available after the draft" view (it re-checks every 15 s) and fills in by itself the moment the draft is complete. After the draft a failed refresh (chat/presence poll every 30 s, scores and tournament data every 5 min) keeps the last good view on screen and only adds a small "Offline: showing the last saved view. Retrying…" note, which goes away when the store answers again.
- **Banner photo**: default `assets/hero-banner.jpg` (cover, centred); a CSS gradient is only the fallback if the image fails. The **commissioner** (secret remembered on this device or opened with `#cm=`) sees *Change photo* / *Reset to default*; nobody else does. The photo is resized and JPEG-compressed in the browser (`F.fitHero`, at most 150 KB), kept in its own small key (`...-hero`, never polled with the draft) with a write-then-verify, read once and cached on the device for 10 minutes. Anything read from the store is sanitised (`F.heroOk`: only `data:image/jpeg|png|webp` within the size cap, otherwise the default) and applied through DOM style properties. Like every other key the store is public, so the commissioner check is a UI rule, not real authorisation (see "Known limits").
- **Two-way navigation**: the room header, the end-of-draft screen, the recap, the League site and the redraft pages link to each other with the league code only. The manager identity (`fbn.me.<code>`) and commissioner secret (`fbn.cm.<code>`) stay in this device's localStorage, so My Team and the commissioner controls are still there after going back and forth. Spectator links add `&watch=1`. No secret is ever put in a link.
- **End of the draft** (draft room): a full-width primary **League site** at the very top, a large *See my team* (spectators: *See the teams*), a small *Draft recap*, the headline, **What's next** (season start, how points work, the January 20, 2027 countdown), *Invite friends to watch* (spectator link) and a *Save to home screen* tip. On a phone a sticky bottom League site button follows (hidden on the Chat tab, hidden on a desktop). The recap page has the same primary and sticky buttons, rosters side by side (scroll on a phone, grid on a desktop), a roster-strength rating and *Best pick* / *Biggest reach*.
- **Draft room tabs** (Board, Teams, My Team, Chat, More) are a sticky tab row at the top (role=tablist, arrow keys move between tabs); there is no bottom bar.

### League site data
Before the draft is complete the page only says **Available after the draft** (with links back to the room). After that it is always populated. `data/scores.json` (empty until the season) feeds the records and points; the invented sample scores live in `data/demo/` and are reachable only through the **DEMO** link in the footer (`league.html?l=<code>&demo=1`): the demo carries a sticky DEMO bar and a DEMO note, and sample files are ignored even if they are copied into `data/`.
- **Standings**: Full Season points = wins by the manager's schools. **Postseason**: NCAA Tournament standings (points per school), the checklist of all 48 schools and the upset list.
- **Games**: Upcoming / Scores for the drafted schools, filter by owner and day, upsets flagged. Times are in the viewer's time zone.
- **Rosters**: pick an owner, see each school with record, points and last games. **All teams**: all 48 schools with owner and record. **Rules**: this app's scoring. **Archive**: empty state (no past seasons yet). **Jan 20, 2027 redraft**: the redraft card (rules, countdown, link to the redraft page).
- **Power rankings** (extra tab): `data/power-rankings.json` (grade badge A+ to D, a short 4-sentence recap per team; one more tap, *Deep dive*, opens the 10-sentence recap and the school list; `aria-expanded`, keyboard accessible). The file is a static analysis of one specific draft, so it is shown only when it describes **this** league: **all 8** managers must match, each by manager or team name **and** by exactly the same set of schools as that manager's roster. A partial match (even 7 of 8, or one school different, or a roster changed by the redraft) shows nothing but *Power rankings appear after the draft is analysed*; mismatched data is never shown. The shipped file belongs to the test league, so a small note says so.
- **Live scores overlay**: while games exist, the page polls the public ESPN scoreboard (`site.api.espn.com`, GET only, nothing is stored or sent) and overlays live scores; this is the only change to the CSP (`connect-src 'self' https://textdb.dev https://site.api.espn.com`). The *Refresh Live Games* link does it on demand.

Links `league.html?l=<code>#games`, `#rosters`, `#teams`, `#postseason`, `#redraft`, `#rules`, `#power` open a tab directly (the old `#standings/tournament` opens Postseason).

**Scoring** (`js/scoring.js`, rules also in `data/FORMAT.md`). Full Season = wins by a manager's schools in regular-season and conference games (ties: win %, then point difference). NCAA Tournament = points per school: in the field 5, play-in win 1, each win 2, upset by seed +4 (the winner has the worse seed; equal seeds are not an upset), Final Four +3, champion +4, missing the field -1 (only once the 64+ team field list is loaded). Ties: more upsets, then more wins. Seeds only; no other data is used. The values are fixed in `js/scoring.js` and can be overridden in `data/tournament.json` (`points`).

**Data** is two static files, read by the browser from the same folder as the page: `data/scores.json` and `data/tournament.json` (format: `data/FORMAT.md`). The files in `data/` that ship are **empty** (no games, no field). The invented sample set (2,548 games, a 68-team field) is in `data/demo/` and is used only by the DEMO link, which marks every screen DEMO. If a file is missing or broken the site says so in plain words (standings at zero, "The tournament has not started", "No games yet") and the team cards still work.

**Refreshing the data.** `python3 tools/refresh_data.py` (Python standard library, no key) reads the ESPN scoreboard day by day, falls back to the NCAA scoreboard relay for a day ESPN fails, keeps only games that involve a school in `assets/teams.js`, and writes both files with `"sample": false`. Useful flags: `--start/--end` for a window, `--merge` to keep older games, `--out DIR` to write somewhere else first, `--self-test` for an offline parser check. Run it, check the diff, commit and push `data/`. The demo files come from `tools/make_sample_data.py` (invented, seeded; writes to `data/demo/`). The refresh was tried live on 2026-03-18..20 (First Four and 1st Round: 34 games) into a temp folder; it has not been run for the 2026-27 season yet because that season has not started.

## Accessibility (draft room)
- Toasts are a polite live region (`role=status`). Dialogs (sheets, the pick list, the spin overlay, the identity overlay) are `role=dialog` with `aria-modal`, move focus in when they open, keep Tab inside, give focus back to the button that opened them and close with Escape. In the spin overlay Escape only hides it; it never stops or cancels a spin. Escape on the pick list never loses a pick (a pick is made only on the confirm sheet).
- Error lines (unlock, identity link, typed confirmations) are `role=alert`; the unread badge has visually hidden text ("3 unread messages"); the board header cells have `scope`; the team search boxes and the chat box have labels; `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off every animation and transition (the spin shows its result without the animation).
- Touch targets are at least 44 px (including reactions, the drag grip and text buttons); the chat box stays pinned to the bottom of the screen and keeps the keyboard open after Send.
- The sticky *League site* button is hidden on short landscape phones (height 500 px or less) and on wide screens; the end-of-draft banner always has the same button.

## Known limits and decisions
- **Friendly locks, not real authentication.** Anyone with a league link can read the league (and, with the code, join an open seat). The commissioner secret and each manager's secret are random 128-bit values that live only in the private links (after the `#`, so they are never sent to a server) and on the device; the league stores only a salted SHA-256 hash of each, so copying the stored data gives nobody a working secret. The app refuses to pick, queue, edit a profile, undo, pause or start without the matching secret. But textdb has no access control: a person who writes the store's keys by hand, outside the app, can still overwrite anything. That cannot be fixed without a backend that checks who is writing (the Supabase swap).
- **Depends on textdb.dev**: free, third-party, no SLA, ~1–2 s per request from some networks, whole-document last-write-wins. Data can disappear if the service does.
- **Polling, no push**: the room refreshes every few seconds; no notifications when the app is closed.
- Every new league has exactly 8 manager seats (fixed; there is no setting for it, and the wizard and League settings only offer Teams each, up to 15 and never more than the 162-team pool allows for 8 managers). A league saved earlier with another number of seats still opens and runs with its own number (its settings sheet shows the number as fixed text). The draft can start only when exactly 8 managers have joined (so a league saved earlier with another number of seats cannot start; it can still be opened and watched); late joiners after the start are spectators.
- **ESPN logos are hot-linked** from ESPN's CDN (with a colour-chip fallback if they fail). That is a rights/availability risk; replace with licensed or self-made marks before a public launch.
- **Feed terms are uncertain.** The refresh script reads ESPN's public scoreboard and the NCAA relay (ncaa-api.henrygd.me) without a key. Those are undocumented/community feeds with no stated terms for this use and no guarantee they keep working; check their terms before a public launch. The site itself never calls them (only the maintainer's script does).
- **No scores until you refresh.** The shipped `data/` files are empty. The real refresh was only tried on last season's First Four and 1st Round days.
- **21 extra teams.** `assets/teams.js` has 162 schools: 141 come from the hardened-draft snapshot, and 21 more were added by hand (`tools/teammeta.py`: Miami (OH), Oklahoma, UC Santa Barbara, Furman, UIC, Marquette, Montana State, Dayton, Stephen F. Austin, St. Thomas, Santa Clara, Western Kentucky, Clemson, Merrimack, Creighton, Baylor, West Virginia, Providence, Central Arkansas, Queens University, Long Island University). Their colours, logos and power ranks should be checked by a person before launch.
- **Trademark**: "NCAA" in the product name is a registered trademark. Review the name (and the team names/logos) before any public release.
- The league code is the only index (no lookup service): lose the link and the code, lose the league. Photos make the profile key larger (≈25 KB each).
- Points only: nothing in the app tracks anything but picks, scores and chat. Humor is off.
- **League site data is refreshed by hand**: the site never calls ESPN itself, so scores are only as fresh as the last `tools/refresh_data.py` run and commit. Until the first refresh a real league shows no scores.
- **Seeds come from the scoreboard feed.** A school's seed is read from game data; until the First Four / 1st Round games are listed, a school outside the first games has no seed (it still gets entry points from `data/tournament.json`). Games and schools not in `assets/teams.js` are dropped. NIT and other postseason events are not scored.
- The Full Season standings count regular-season and conference games only; NCAA tournament games count only in tournament mode.
- Standings are computed in the browser from the data files, so a very large season file (about 0.5 MB for the demo set) is downloaded on each visit (network-first service worker).
- The hosted store keeps a league's data until the service drops it. The app has no delete-league button (textdb has no delete); the commissioner can blank the keys by hand, see the key table.
- Stage 3 hook: `FBNLeague.load(id)` plus `FBNScore` give everything a later page needs.

## Before public release
These are open items that need a person's decision or permission. None is a code bug.
1. **The name.** "NCAA" is a registered trademark, and the product name and every page header say it. Use descriptive wording such as "college basketball" and "March tournament" in the name, keep "NCAA" only in body text, and add a line "not affiliated with or endorsed by the NCAA or any school". The same care applies to school names and colours.
2. **School logos.** The 162 logos are loaded from `https://a.espncdn.com/...` at view time. ESPN's terms do not allow this use, the addresses can change or be blocked, and every viewer's IP address reaches ESPN. Replace them with text chips, logos used with permission, or licensed art. The coloured-chip fallback already works without them.
3. **The shared store.** textdb.dev is a free third-party service: no login, anyone with a key can read or overwrite it, no service promise, no privacy policy, no deletion. Names and photos of real people are saved there. Get the owner's permission for this request volume (polling is now light, see "How it works"), or move to the planned backend, before real use.
4. **Privacy and children.** The join form now says that names, team names and photos are saved in a shared store anyone with the league link can read. A terms and privacy page is still needed, and a way to delete a league's data. Think about photos of minors.

## Redraft (January 20, 2027)
After the first draft is complete, the league page has a **Redraft** button (`redraft.html`). Rules, in plain words:
1. The pick order follows the league standings **as of January 20, 2027** (games up to and including that day). The best record picks first; it is a snake.
2. The owner in **last place** chooses where they pick (any position). No choice means they pick last.
3. Standings use win percentage. Same percentage: the owner with **fewer wins** ranks lower. Then point difference, then seat order.
4. Each owner may **drop up to 2 teams** (optional). Dropped teams go back into the pool; the owner drafts one team for each team dropped.

How it runs: the commissioner opens the redraft (standings are saved in `meta.redraft`), every owner saves their choices with their own manager secret (key `...-rd-<managerId>`), then the commissioner presses Start. Start moves the league to a new epoch (`epoch + 1`, live, snake): picks live under `...-e<epoch+1>-p<n>`, kept teams are listed in `meta.redraft.keep`, and slots of owners who dropped nothing (or whose drops are used up) are skipped automatically. The normal draft room runs the draft (clock, auto-pick, undo, end early; Reset is not offered). A league can be redrafted once. Old leagues without a `redraft` field behave as before.
Known limits: the league page still counts a team's wins for its current owner for the whole season (it does not split a team's record before and after January 20, 2027).
