How to run a league

A short guide in plain words. Two parts: one for the commissioner (the person who sets the league up) and one for managers (everyone who drafts). Spectators are covered at the end.

Part 1 · Commissioner

1. Create the league

  1. On the home page tap Create a league.
  2. Answer one question per screen: league name, your name and team name, how many managers (4 to 12), draft style, draft time, who picks first, time per pick, and how many teams each manager gets.
  3. Snake reverses the order every round. Linear keeps the same order every round.
  4. Check the summary screen. You can change any answer there. Nothing is created until you confirm.

2. Save and share the links

  • Invite link (and QR code): send it to your managers. Anyone with it can join while there are free spots.
  • Draft room link and spectator link: the room for everyone, and a watch-only version.
  • Your private commissioner link: unlocks the commissioner tools on a phone. Save it somewhere safe. It cannot be recovered. Do not share it.
  • Your private manager link: gets you back into your own team on another phone.
  • Tap Copy reminder message for a ready note with the league name, the draft time and the invite link.

Private links show as dots on screen on purpose. Use the copy button.

3. Fill the lobby and set the order

  • Managers appear in the lobby as they join. You can rename or remove a manager from More.
  • Order: Randomize, spin the wheel, or move people yourself (drag the handle, or tap the up and down arrows).
  • The order can change until the draft starts. After that it is locked.

4. Draft day

  • Tap Start draft when everyone is in, or let it start by itself at the time you set (see the limits at the bottom).
  • If fewer people joined than you planned, the draft uses the managers who are there.
  • Each manager has a pick clock. A warning shows and sounds when 15 seconds are left.

5. Pause, undo and other tools

Open More in the room. The tools are there once your commissioner link has unlocked this phone.

  • Pause and Resume the clock.
  • Undo last pick. The dialog names the pick first. If the board changed while you were looking, it asks you to look again instead of removing the wrong pick.
  • Change the time per pick, turn pick notes on or off, End the draft early, or Reset (typed confirmation for both).
  • No unlock yet on this phone? Open your commissioner link, or paste it under More → Unlock.

6. Managers who are away, and auto-pick

  • Tap a manager under More → Managers and choose Mark away — auto-pick covers them. Auto-pick then makes their picks until you mark them back.
  • Auto-pick takes the first available team from that manager's queue, then the best power-ranked team left.
  • The same thing happens when a manager's clock runs out, if auto-pick on timeout is on.
  • If the tab was closed when the time came, the app shows a one-tap button: Start the draft now or Make the auto-pick now.

7. Locked out of the commissioner tools?

Open your private commissioner link again. Or open the room, go to More → Unlock and paste the link (or just the secret code at the end of it). If you have lost it, it cannot be recovered. That is why step 2 says to save it.

Part 2 · Managers

1. Join

  1. Open the invite link from your commissioner, or type the league code on the home page and tap Join.
  2. Enter your team name and your name. A photo is optional. You can use an emoji or your initial instead.
  3. Your name, team and photo are saved in a shared online store. Anyone who has the league link can see them.
  4. After you join you get a private manager link. Copy it and keep it.

2. Wait for the order

The lobby shows who is in and, once it is set, the draft order. The commissioner sets it before the start. It cannot change after that.

3. Draft day

  • Open the draft room on your phone and keep it open. The top banner shows who is on the clock.
  • When it is your turn, tap Pick a team, find a team (search by name, mascot or conference), tap it and confirm.
  • If someone takes a team first, the app tells you and you pick another.
  • Under Teams use the star to mark teams you like. Use Q+ to add teams to your queue.
  • My Team shows your queue and your picks. Chat is separate from the pick feed.

4. Auto-pick

Switch auto-pick on under My Team and the app picks for you from your queue, then by power rank. It also picks if your clock runs out. Auto-pick only runs while someone has the room open, so do not count on it if nobody is looking.

5. New phone, or you closed the page?

  • Open your private manager link. You are back in as yourself.
  • Or open the room and, when it asks who you are, paste the link (or its secret code).
  • There is no list of names to tap. Without your link nobody can act as you.
  • If the commissioner removed you, the room says so. You can still watch.

Spectators

Anyone with the spectator link sees the board, the picks and the chat, but cannot pick or write. People who open the room after the draft has started are offered this view.

After the draft

  • Final rosters and recap shows every team, with Export CSV and Export JSON.
  • League standings is the league site. It has Standings, Teams and Games, with a Full Season and NCAA Tournament toggle.
  • Before the season starts it says Standings start when the season starts. There is an Open the demo link that shows invented games, clearly marked DEMO, so you can see how it will look.

Known limits

A page must be open for auto-start, auto-pick and the pick clock to happen. There is no server of ours running them. If every tab is closed, nothing happens until someone opens the room.

The links and secrets are a friendly lock for a friends league, not real security. League data lives in a public shared store, so share the league link only with people you trust.
