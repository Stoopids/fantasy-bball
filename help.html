How to run a league

Short and plain. Every league has exactly 8 managers: the commissioner and 7 friends.

CommissionerManagersWatchingAfterKnown limits

Commissioner

  1. Create: on the home page tap Create a league and answer each question. Check the summary, then confirm.
  2. Share: send the Invite link (or the QR code) to your 7 friends. Save your private commissioner link. It cannot be recovered, so do not share it.
  3. Order: in the lobby tap Randomize, spin the wheel (one spin only; everyone sees it), or move people yourself.
  4. Start draft: it works only when all 8 managers have joined. Until then the lobby says “Waiting for N more managers” and the button stays off. Tap it when everyone is in, or let it start at the time you set (a timed start also waits for all 8).
  5. During the draft (More in the room): Pause, Undo the last pick, or Mark away so auto-pick covers someone who is gone. Locked out? Open your commissioner link again.

Managers

  1. Join: open the invite link, add your team name and your name. A photo is optional.
  2. Save your private manager link. It brings you back into your team on a new phone or after closing the page.
  3. Pick: keep the room open. On your turn tap Pick a team, choose one and confirm. Add teams to your queue with Q+.
  4. Auto-pick: switch it on under My Team and the app picks for you if you run out of time.

How your queue works

Your queue never picks by itself. On your turn you pick. If your clock runs out (league auto-pick on), or you turn on Auto-pick for me (picks right away when your turn comes), we take your top available queued team, then the best-ranked team left.

Watching

Anyone with the spectator link can watch the board and chat. A spectator cannot pick.

After the draft

The Draft recap shows every roster, the Power rankings and lets you export CSV or JSON. League site appears by itself when the draft ends, with Standings, Games, Rosters, Bets, All teams, Postseason, Redraft, Archive and Rules. Use Draft room and Draft recap there to go back and forth.

Redraft, January 20, 2027: tap Redraft on the League site. Each owner may drop up to 2 teams. The draft follows the standings as of that date, and the last-place owner chooses where to pick.

Bets: pushup challenges between managers. The other manager accepts, and the loser owes pushups.

Known limits

  • A page must be open for the clock, auto-start and auto-pick to work.
  • Links are a friendly lock, not real security.
  • League data is kept in a public shared store, so share links only with people you trust.
