How to run a league

Short and plain. Every league has exactly 8 managers: the commissioner and 7 friends.

CommissionerManagersWatchingAfterKnown limits

Commissioner

  1. Create: on the home page tap Create a league and answer each question. Check the summary, then confirm.
  2. Share: send the Invite link (or the QR code) to your 7 friends. Save your private commissioner link. It cannot be recovered, so do not share it.
  3. Order: in the lobby tap Randomize, spin the wheel (one spin only; everyone sees it), or move people yourself.
  4. Start draft: it works only when all 8 managers have joined. Until then the lobby says “Waiting for N more managers” and the button stays off. Tap it when everyone is in, or let it start at the time you set (a timed start also waits for all 8).
  5. During the draft (More in the room): Pause, Undo the last pick, or Mark away so auto-pick covers someone who is gone. Locked out? Open your commissioner link again.

Managers

  1. Join: open the invite link, add your team name and your name. A photo is optional.
  2. Save your private manager link. It brings you back into your team on a new phone or after closing the page.
  3. Pick: keep the room open. On your turn tap Pick a team, choose one and confirm. Add teams to your queue with Q+.
  4. Auto-pick: switch it on under My Team and the app picks for you if you run out of time.

How your queue works

Your queue never picks by itself. On your turn you pick. If your clock runs out (league auto-pick on), or you turn on Auto-pick for me (picks right away when your turn comes), we take your top available queued team, then the best-ranked team left.

Watching

Anyone with the spectator link can watch the board and chat. A spectator cannot pick.

How to bet

Where to start

On a wide screen, Write it Up or Request shows both forms side by side. On a phone there are three tabs: Write it Up, Request, and All Bets. One form shows at a time. Quick bet on an upcoming game that has not started opens Request with that side filled in, including a game with no owner and no odds. Live and final games do not show Quick bet. Nothing is posted until you submit. Amounts are pushups. The Bets number is only bets you have not seen on All Bets yet. It disappears when you open that list. The All Bets number is only new requests you can see, and it disappears the same way. Opening Write it Up or Request does not clear either number. Changing a filter does not clear them.

Write it Up

Use this when the bet is already agreed. It goes straight onto All Bets. Pick who you’re against, write the bet, choose the amount and the odds, then 5. When can this be settled? That date, End of season, or Not sure yet is for sorting only. Tap Place bet, check it, then Yes, place it. The formatted text is copied only after that save succeeds.

Request a bet

Use this when nothing is agreed yet. Pick who it is open to. Leave them unpicked, or choose All, and it is open to the other people in the league. A shorter list is only those invitees. Set how many can accept, and when the invitation expires. Expiry is not the settlement estimate. Then 7. When can this be settled?, including End game on a wide screen. That label is kept and does not settle the bet. Tap Request bet. It stays under Open requests at the top of All Bets until someone accepts, it expires, or you delete or close it.

Who sees an open request

The page shows an open request to the person who sent it, and to invitees who can still take it. Other people do not get that card or a request badge. A guest does not see those cards. Accept still only works for an invitee. This is how the page filters the list. The request record itself is still read as one shared list, so the page is not a private lock.

How the All Bets filters work

This update only

This is how the filters work in this held update. They are not on the public site yet. Nothing here settles a bet by itself.

Five controls

Everyone / a person and search stay. The old long status list is now three controls that all apply at once. A bet has to pass every one of them. Status is All, Open, Settled, Wash, or Cancelled. Action needed is Any, My turn, or Their turn. When is Any time, Upcoming, Today, Past settle date, End game, End of season, or Not sure yet. The page opens on Open, Any action, and Any time.

Status

Open is still open. Settled has a winner. Wash is a push: nobody owes anything. Cancelled was withdrawn or declined. All includes every one of those.

Whose turn

My turn means there is a real step with your name on it: answer someone else’s correction, confirm a score that has already been proposed, or enter the result because that bet’s game is final and no score was proposed. A sorting date does not do this. A game later today, and a game still being played, are not your turn. A correction you still have to answer counts even if that game has not been played. Their turn is the same kind of step for the other person, on a bet you are in. An open bet is not “their turn” only because they wrote it. The number on My turn or Their turn is how many bets match that choice together with the other controls. It is not the yellow new-bet badge, and it is not the new-request badge. Those unseen numbers clear when you open All Bets. Changing a filter does not clear them.

When

Today, Upcoming, and Past settle date use this device’s calendar day, the same day the page already uses for sorting. They are not Eastern Time and not the tip-off. Past settle date means the sorting date is before today. The bet stays open until someone enters the result. End of season and End game are only those labels. No season-end date and no game-end time is invented, and neither one settles the bet.

Requests stay separate

Open requests stay in their own list at the top of All Bets. Status, Action needed, and When do not try to classify a request. Everyone, Me, a named person, and search do apply to that list, but only after the page has already hidden requests you cannot see. You see a request you sent, or one you were invited to and can still take. You never see someone else’s request just because you searched their name. A guest does not see those cards.

Three examples

Open + My turn: the game is final and you still need to confirm the proposed score, or enter the result because the final did not propose one. It shows. A game today that has not started, and a game that is still being played, do not. Next week does not either.

Settled + Their turn: you asked to correct the result, and the person who posted it has not answered. It stays settled until they do.

Open + Any + End of season: those bets are grouped by that label. They do not settle when the season ends, because no end date is on file.

After the draft

The Draft recap shows every roster, the Power rankings and lets you export CSV or JSON. League site appears by itself when the draft ends, with Standings, Games, Rosters, Bets, All teams, Redraft, Archive and Rules. Use Draft room and Draft recap there to go back and forth.

Redraft, January 20, 2027: tap Redraft on the League site. Each owner may drop up to 2 teams. The draft follows the standings as of that date, and the last-place owner chooses where to pick.

Bets: pushup challenges between managers. The other manager accepts, and the loser owes pushups.

Known limits

  • A page must be open for the clock, auto-start and auto-pick to work.
  • Links are a friendly lock, not real security.
  • League data is kept in a public shared store, so share links only with people you trust.
